Tune how VisionLingo works for you

When on, VisionLingo automatically identifies and speaks aloud whatever's centered in view every few seconds — no tap needed. Tap objects any time for translations, saving, and pinning.

Runs fully on-device. No account, no server, no cost — detection uses TensorFlow.js, pronunciation uses your browser's speech engine, translation uses a free public API.

How to use VisionLingo

🌐
Select a language

Open Settings and choose your target language.

📷
Point your camera

Boxes appear automatically around recognizable objects.

＋
Tap, then pin

Tap a box (or anywhere) to identify it, then tap "Pin label on screen" to anchor it.

🔊
Hear & save

Tap a pinned label to hear its pronunciation or save it to your collection.

✋
Remove a label

Long-press any pinned label, or the word card, to remove it.
